CityVoice

Report a problem in your street to your city by talking to Alexa: a pothole, a broken streetlight, a missed trash pickup. Alexa asks what the city needs to know, reads the report back, and sends it when you say yes. If a neighbor already reported it, you can add your support instead.

CityVoice speaks Open311, the open standard many US cities use for 311 requests. This pilot runs on demonstration data for Washington DC, and reads San Francisco's real requests to show the same code on a real city. Reports go to a sandbox, never to a city.

Try saying:

  • "There's a huge pothole at 14th and U Street."
  • "The streetlight in front of my house is out."
  • "What's happening with my reports?"

Try it in the Alexa+ simulator

For cities

Every report filed through CityVoice is published in the Open311 GeoReport v2 format, the one 311 systems already read: the requests and the services. It is exactly what a city would receive, minus who filed it.
