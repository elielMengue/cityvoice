CityVoice privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

CityVoice lets you report problems in your street, like a pothole or a broken streetlight, by talking to Alexa, and lets you ask where your reports stand. This page explains what we receive when you use it, what we do with it, and what we never do.

CityVoice is a pilot built for the Amazon Build, Ship, Shape hackathon. It runs on demonstration data for Washington DC, and on San Francisco's public 311 requests, which it only reads. Reports are not sent to any city: they go to a sandbox that imitates a city's 311 system.

What we receive

  • Your account link. When you link CityVoice to Alexa, you pick a demonstration resident. We keep which resident you picked, so your reports stay yours. We never receive your Amazon password, your name, your email address or your real home address.
  • What you report. Alexa passes on the words you use to describe the problem, the place you name, and your answers to the city's questions. You hear the full report before anything is sent, and nothing is sent without a clear yes.
  • What you support. When you add your support to a neighbor's report, we record that you did, so you are only counted once.

We do not receive your voice recordings. Alexa turns your speech into text, and Amazon's own privacy notice covers that part.

What we do with it

We use what you tell us for one thing: to file and follow your reports. A report you start but do not send is deleted after 15 minutes. Reports you send stay in the sandbox so you can ask about them later.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use what you say to train any model.

Who else sees it

  • Your neighbors can hear that a problem was reported near them, with its place and how many people support it. They never hear who reported it.
  • Anyone can read the reports filed through CityVoice in its public Open311 feed, the way many cities publish their 311 requests: the kind of problem, the place, your description and where it stands. The feed never says who filed a report.
  • Cloudflare hosts CityVoice. To deliver and protect the service, it processes technical data such as IP addresses. We use them only to slow down abuse, and we do not store them.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the street maps shown on screens. Our server fetches the map images itself, so OpenStreetMap does not see your device.

How long we keep it

Unsent drafts are deleted after 15 minutes. Because this is a pilot, all demonstration data, including your reports, is reset from time to time and will be deleted when the pilot ends.

Your choices

You can unlink CityVoice from the Alexa app at any time. Alexa then drops its access, and can no longer act for you in CityVoice. To ask us to delete your reports or your link sooner, contact us.

Children

CityVoice is meant for adults reporting problems in their street. It is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. For any question about your data, open an issue on the CityVoice project page.
